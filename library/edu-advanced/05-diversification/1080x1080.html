Portfolio Diversification

Huwag ilagay lahat sa isang coin. Kahit mag-crash ang isa, hindi lahat mawawala.

  • 50% BTC/ETH — stable core
  • 30% altcoins — growth
  • 20% stablecoins — reserve
  • Adjust based sa risk tolerance mo
📌 Example
Basic Pinoy split: 50/30/20. Ang goal: protected ka kahit volatile ang market.
Crypto trading is risky. Use money you can afford to lose.
