Take-Profit Strategies

Huwag mag-hold forever — set TP levels. Worst case: profit na sana, naging loss pa.

  • Fixed % — sell 50% at 2x
  • Trailing stop — auto-adjust pataas
  • Scale out — 25% at each target
  • Lock in gains, wag maging greedy
