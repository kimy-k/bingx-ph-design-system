Paano Gumagana ang Liquidation?

Kapag bumaba ang margin mo below maintenance level, automatic close ang position mo — liquidation.

  • Mas mataas ang leverage, mas malapit ang liquidation price
  • Higher leverage = smaller move bago ma-liquidate
  • Lower leverage = mas malaki ang buffer mo
  • Laging mag-set ng stop-loss bago pa mangyari
📌 Example
10x leverage, ₱10,000 position = ₱1,000 margin. ~10% move against you = LIQUIDATED.
Crypto trading is risky. Use money you can afford to lose.
