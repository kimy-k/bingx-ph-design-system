📘 BASICS

Ano ang Slippage?

Slippage = difference between expected price at actual fill price.

  • Nangyayari kapag mababa ang liquidity
  • O kapag mabilis ang market movement
  • Mas malaki ang order, mas malaki ang slippage
  • Use limit orders para controlled ang entry
📌 Example
Tip: Limit orders instead of market orders = controlled ang entry price mo, walang surprise fill.
Crypto trading is risky. Use money you can afford to lose.
