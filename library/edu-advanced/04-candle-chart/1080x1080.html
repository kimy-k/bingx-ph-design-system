Paano Mag-Read ng Candle Chart

Ito ang foundation ng technical analysis. Bawat candle = price movement sa isang time period.

  • Green candle = presyo umakyat
  • Red candle = presyo bumaba
  • Body = open to close range
  • Wick/shadow = high and low ng period
  • Long wick sa baba = buying pressure
  • Long wick sa taas = selling pressure
Wick = high/low
Body = open→close
Crypto trading is risky. Use money you can afford to lose.
