Ano ang Funding Rate?

Funding rate = bayad ng longs sa shorts (o vice versa) every 8 hours. Para ma-keep ang perp price malapit sa spot.

  • Positive rate = longs pay shorts
  • Negative rate = shorts pay longs
  • Sobrang taas na funding = maraming naka-long — risky
  • Check mo funding bago mag-hold overnight
Crypto trading is risky. Use money you can afford to lose.
