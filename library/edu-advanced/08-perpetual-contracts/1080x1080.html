⚡ FUTURES

Perpetual Contracts

Perps = futures na walang expiry date. Hindi mo kailangang i-roll over.

  • Naka-peg sa spot price via funding rate
  • Long = bet na aakyat
  • Short = bet na bababa
  • Most popular instrument sa crypto trading
📌 Example
Hindi tulad ng traditional futures, walang expiry ang perps — pwede mong hawakan basta may margin ka.
Crypto trading is risky. Use money you can afford to lose.
