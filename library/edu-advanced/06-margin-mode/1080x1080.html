Cross vs Isolated Margin

Dalawang margin mode sa futures — malaking difference sa risk exposure mo.

  • Cross = buong balance mo ang collateral
  • Isolated = position margin lang ang at risk
  • Beginners: ALWAYS use isolated
  • Cross is for advanced traders lang
📌 Example
Isolated limits ang loss sa isang position. Kung pumalpak, position margin lang ang mawawala — hindi buong account.
Crypto trading is risky. Use money you can afford to lose.
