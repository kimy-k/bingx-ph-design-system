Paano Gumagana ang Airdrops?

Airdrop = free tokens from projects. Usually para sa early users, testnet participants, o holders.

  • Para sa early users o testnet participants
  • Legit airdrops NEVER ask for seed phrase
  • Hindi rin humihingi ng deposit
  • Check official channels only
📌 Example
Free money exists — pero may scam versions din. Kung may humihingi ng seed phrase o deposit, scam yan.
Crypto trading is risky. Use money you can afford to lose.
